## 🅰️ FE-TextMaster (`FE-TextMaster.jsx`)

**FE-TextMaster** is a complete Typography & Text Motion Engine packed into a single dockable panel:

### 🚀 1. 100 Unique Text Animation Presets (10 Categories)
- **1. Bounce & Elastic Motion** (Overshoot Scale, Gravity Drop, Elastic Tracking, Wobbly Rotation, etc.)
- **2. Kinetic & Typography Dynamics** (Typewriter, Decaying Wiggle, Inertial Snap, Word Pop, etc.)
- **3. Glitch & Digital Distortion** (Binary Code Matrix, RGB Split, Signal Flicker, VCR Tracking, etc.)
- **4. UI & Digital HUD FX** (Cursor Tracking Typewriter, Progress Counter, Code Decryptor, Neon Strike, etc.)
- **5. Wave, Fluid & Physics** (Sinusoidal Wave, Fluid Ripple, Jelly Distortion, Pendulum Swing, etc.)
- **6. 3D Space & Isometric** (3D Flip & Bounce, Isometric Slide, 3D Helix, Z-Space Elastic Drop, etc.)
- **7. Particle, Shatter & Assembly** (Gravity Disintegration, Reverse Shatter, Explosive Push, Dust Dissolve, etc.)
- **8. Gaming & Social Media Style** (Score Popup Elastic, Damage Number Float, Subscribe Shake, Level Up Banner, etc.)
- **9. Mathematical & Procedural** (Fibonacci Scale, Damped Oscillator, Perlin Noise Shift, Velocity Elasticity, etc.)
- **10. Minimal, Clean & Corporate** (Smooth Overshoot Slide, Subtle Tracking, Line Mask Unfold, Elegant Rise, etc.)

### ⚡ 2. Special FX & Expression Controls
- **Typewriter with Blinking Cursor**: `_` or `|` live cursor animation.
- **Digital Code Decryptor**: Random character shuffling resolving into final text.
- **Decaying Text Wiggle**: High-frequency initial shake dampening smoothly.

### 🎯 3. Per-Character Anchor Point Matrix
- Snaps character-level transform anchor points across 9 positions (`Top-Left`, `Top-Center`, `Top-Right`, `Mid-Left`, `Center`, `Mid-Right`, `Bot-Left`, `Baseline`, `Bot-Right`) so scale & rotation animate from any character edge.

### 🛠 4. Split Text Engine & Case Converter
- **Split Text**: Instantly split text layers by **Characters**, **Words**, or **Lines** into separate individual layers.
- **Case Converter**: Convert text to `UPPERCASE`, `lowercase`, or `Title Case`.
- **Text to Shapes**: Convert text layers into editable vector Shape Layers in 1 click.

## 🚀 Installation & Setup

1. Download **[`FE-TextMaster.zip`](https://github.com/FramEmpire/FE-Scripts-AfterEffects/raw/main/FE-TextMaster.zip)** or **[`ShapeMaster.zip`](https://github.com/FramEmpire/FE-Scripts-AfterEffects/raw/main/ShapeMaster.zip)**.
2. Extract the `.jsx` file and copy it into your ScriptUI Panels directory:
   - **macOS**: `/Applications/Adobe After Effects <version>/Scripts/ScriptUI Panels/`
   - **Windows**: `C:\Program Files\Adobe\Adobe After Effects <version>\Support Files\Scripts\ScriptUI Panels\`
3. Restart After Effects.
4. Launch from top menu: **Window > FE-TextMaster.jsx** (or **Window > ShapeMaster.jsx**).
